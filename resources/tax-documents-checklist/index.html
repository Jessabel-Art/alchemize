Tax Documents Checklist

Use this category-based review to identify common records before tax preparation begins.

Not every item applies to every return. The purpose of this list is to help you recognize relevant categories and ask what is needed for your situation.

Income and payment records

  • Employment, contract, retirement, investment, or other income statements
  • Business income summaries and supporting transaction records
  • Applicable estimated-tax payment confirmations
  • Records for other income that may not appear on a standard form

Expenses and potential adjustments

Gather dated, legible support for expenses that may be relevant. Examples can include eligible business costs, education information, charitable contributions, healthcare-related records, or property information. Applicability must be reviewed rather than assumed.

Prior-year and change records

  • Prior-year federal and applicable state returns
  • Notices or correspondence received from a tax authority
  • Information about a move, marriage, dependent change, property transaction, or new business
  • Carryforward schedules or other continuing tax records

Before sharing documents

Check that pages are complete, names and reporting periods are correct, and amended forms are clearly identified. Use only the secure document-handling method provided for the engagement.

Do not submit tax documents or identity information through the public contact form.
