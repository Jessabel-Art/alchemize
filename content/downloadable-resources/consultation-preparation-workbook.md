# Records to consider

Bring the records that explain the fact, the question, the deadline, or the decision. You do not need every file in your life.

## Bring the record that explains the fact

Examples: notice, policy, agreement, prior return, registration, invoice, current workflow, correspondence, statement, account summary.
[ ] Tax records or notices relevant to the issue
[ ] Insurance summaries or current policy information
[ ] Complete, unsigned document packet and requesting-party instructions
[ ] Correspondence that explains what changed or what was requested

## Bring the record that explains the question

Examples: conflicting instructions, missing information, deadline, prior recommendation, current requirement, or approval condition.
[ ] Documents tied to the decision you are trying to make
[ ] A current workflow, process, or system summary
[ ] Records showing what has already happened
[ ] Records showing what is still missing or unresolved
