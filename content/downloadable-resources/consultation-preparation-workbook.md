# Start with the situation

A useful consultation usually starts with the responsibility or decision, not a pile of documents. This page clarifies what is happening, what is blocked, and what outcome would actually help -- the raw material for the brief you will build by the end.

MICRO: START HERE|Write these as you would explain them to a friend, not formally. You will tighten the language later.

# Records to bring

Bring the records that explain the fact, the question, the deadline, or the decision -- not every file in your life.

## Bring what explains the fact and the question

[ ] Notices, policies, agreements, or prior filings relevant to the issue
[ ] Correspondence that explains what changed or was requested
[ ] Documents tied to the decision you are trying to make
[ ] Records showing what has already happened, and what is still unresolved
