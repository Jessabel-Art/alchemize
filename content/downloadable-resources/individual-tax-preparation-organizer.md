# What happened this year?

The easiest way to find your missing documents is not to think about tax forms. It is to think about your year: did you change jobs, move, get married, buy a home, start freelancing, or receive a notice? Each answer points to a document category below.

MICRO: START HERE|Check anything that applies, even loosely. You can sort out exactly which document you need once you know which categories apply to you.

## Life events checklist

[ ] Changed jobs, retired, or had a major income change

[ ] Got married, divorced, or had a dependent join or leave the household
[ ] Bought or sold a home, or had investment activity

# What that points to

Each life event above usually points to a specific document category. Use this as your search list, then confirm what you actually have.

## Life event to document map

| If this happened... | Look for... |
| Job change or major income change | W-2s, final pay stub, severance records |
| Move | Updated address on all accounts, moving expense records if applicable |
| Marriage, divorce, or dependent change | Marriage/divorce records, dependent's SSN, custody documentation |
| Home purchase, sale, or investment activity | Closing statements, brokerage statements, 1099s |
| Self-employment or gig work | Platform payout summaries, expense records, 1099-NEC/K |
| Notice or estimated payments | The notice itself, payment confirmations |

# Final readiness

Before you share anything, confirm your records are complete, organized, and understandable. The goal is not to send everything -- it is to send what explains the facts and the open questions.

[ ] Expected income records reviewed against life events
[ ] Missing records tracked and followed up
[ ] Estimated payments and prior-year return identified
[ ] Notices included and questions written down
[ ] Secure sharing method confirmed
[ ] Direct-deposit information provided only through an approved method
