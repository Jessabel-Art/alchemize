# Tax year snapshot

Start with the year, not with the forms. The easiest way to identify missing documents is to think through what changed during the year: job changes, income, home or property activity, retirement, family changes, financial events, or tax notices.

# Final readiness

Before sharing information, confirm the records are complete, organized, and understandable. The goal is not to send everything. It is to send the records that explain the facts and the questions.
[ ] Expected income records reviewed
[ ] Life changes documented and missing records tracked
[ ] Estimated payments and prior-year return identified
[ ] Notices included and questions written down
[ ] Secure sharing method confirmed
[ ] Direct-deposit information will be provided only through an approved method
