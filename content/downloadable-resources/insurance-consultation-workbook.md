# Consultation overview

The purpose of an insurance discussion is usually not to decide quickly. It is to identify the risk, compare the current picture to the real protection needs, and make sure the right questions are asked before selecting a product or making a change.

# Questions to ask

A good insurance discussion is not just about price. It is about what is actually protected and what the contract does not cover.
[ ] What does the coverage protect, and what is excluded?
[ ] What limits, deductibles, or cost-sharing apply?
[ ] When does coverage begin, and are there waiting periods?
[ ] What changes must be reported?
[ ] What factors could affect pricing?
[ ] What happens when a claim occurs?
[ ] Are there contractual requirements?
[ ] Are there overlaps or gaps to discuss?
