# The idea and the model

A durable business foundation is not only a good idea. It is a clear picture of what the business is, who it serves, what it needs to operate, and who controls the important records and decisions. This workbook walks that path in order.

# Money and customer systems

A business should not rely on memory or informal cash flow alone, and it should not make customers guess how to reach it, get a quote, or pay. This page is really one system in two halves: money in, and the experience that earns it.

## Business money rules

[ ] Business income has a defined destination
[ ] Business purchases follow a defined payment process
[ ] Receipts and source records have a defined storage location
[ ] Owner contributions and reimbursements are easy to trace
[ ] Deposits and invoice activity are reconciled to the business records
[ ] Financial access is controlled by the business, not only by a person
