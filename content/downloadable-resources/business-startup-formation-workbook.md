# Financial foundation

A business should not rely on memory or informal cash flow alone. The financial foundation is really a system for where money goes, how records are stored, and who is responsible for review.

## Business money rules

[ ] Business income has a defined destination
[ ] Business purchases follow a defined payment process
[ ] Receipts and source records have a defined storage location
[ ] Owner contributions and reimbursements are easy to trace
[ ] Deposits and invoice activity are reconciled to the business records
[ ] Financial access is controlled by the business, not only by a person

## Business ownership test

For each critical account, ask:
[ ] Is the business the account owner?
[ ] Can more than one authorized person recover it?
[ ] Is billing controlled by the business?
[ ] Is MFA enabled where supported?
[ ] Is the renewal date documented?
[ ] Could the business continue if the current vendor, contractor, or owner disappeared?
