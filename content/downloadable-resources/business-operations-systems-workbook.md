# Digital system inventory

Technology should support operations, not add another layer of confusion. For each system, ask whether it is still doing real work, whether the cost is known, and whether the business -- not just one person -- actually owns it.

## Cost and access check

[ ] Is this system still used regularly, by more than one task?
[ ] Does it duplicate another tool or workflow already in use?
[ ] Is the renewal date known and documented somewhere other than memory?
[ ] Is the account business-owned, with business billing?
[ ] Is recovery access available to an authorized backup person?
[ ] Could the business keep operating if the current owner left tomorrow?
WATCH: Account ownership|Core business systems should not depend solely on an employee, contractor, or former vendor's personal account. Confirm business-controlled ownership, recovery, billing, and backup access for each one.
