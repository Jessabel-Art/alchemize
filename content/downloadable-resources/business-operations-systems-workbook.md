## Workflow map

Inquiry → Qualification → Consultation → Intake → Agreement → Payment → Service → Completion → Follow-up

## Friction signals

[ ] Work starts before agreement or payment is confirmed
[ ] Status questions are repeated because nobody knows the current state
[ ] Files are lost in email or a personal account
[ ] Someone is the only person who understands a process
[ ] A process has no clear confirmation step
CALLOUT: Look for handoff failures|Delays often occur where responsibility moves between people or systems. Identify the trigger, owner, expected output, and confirmation for each handoff.

## Cost and access check

[ ] Is this system still used regularly?
[ ] Does it duplicate another tool or workflow?
[ ] Is the renewal date known and documented?
[ ] Is the account business-owned?
[ ] Is recovery access available to an authorized backup person?
[ ] Can the business still access its data if the current owner leaves?
CALLOUT: Account ownership|Core business systems should not depend solely on an employee, contractor, or former vendor's personal account. Confirm business-controlled ownership, recovery, billing, and backup access.
