# Before your appointment

A notary appointment works best when the document, signers, identification, and instructions are organized before arrival. A short review now prevents delays and avoids the most common appointment issues.

## Document readiness

[ ] The complete document and all pages are present
[ ] Unexplained blanks have been addressed with the requesting organization
[ ] Requesting-organization instructions are available
[ ] The document has been reviewed for completeness
[ ] The requested notarial act is identified if supplied by the requesting party
[ ] The document has not been signed in advance when presence is required
CALLOUT: Important boundary|Do not ask the notary to choose the notarial act or advise whether the document is legally appropriate. Contact the document's recipient, issuing organization, or a qualified legal professional when instructions are unclear.

# Identification, signers, and business capacity

The right identification and the correct signer capacity matter just as much as the document itself. A short review before the appointment can prevent a wasted trip or an incomplete notarization.

## Identification and signers

[ ] Each required signer will be present
[ ] Each signer has acceptable current identification
[ ] Document names and identification have been compared where applicable
[ ] Secondary identification is available if requested

## Witnesses

[ ] Confirm whether witnesses are required
[ ] Confirm who may provide witnesses under the applicable instructions
[ ] Confirm witness identification expectations

## If signing for a business or organization

[ ] Exact business or organization name
[ ] Signer title and capacity
[ ] Authority or supporting information, if requested
[ ] Requesting organization's instructions
[ ] All required business signers
[ ] Delivery recipient

## Representative capacity

[ ] The signer knows the role they are acting in
[ ] The document reflects the correct title or authority if required
[ ] Any supporting authority information is available if requested

# Completion and return

The appointment is not complete simply because the signature is on the page. The final task is to confirm the correct document was completed, copied if needed, and returned according to the instructions.

## Before leaving

[ ] Notarization is complete
[ ] Copies are obtained if needed
[ ] Payment is complete
[ ] Recipient and delivery method are confirmed
[ ] Deadlines and tracking expectations are noted

## Common reasons appointments are delayed

[ ] Missing signer or incomplete document
[ ] Wrong, unavailable, or expired identification
[ ] Unclear witness requirement
[ ] Signature completed before required presence
[ ] Missing requesting-party instructions
[ ] Business capacity or authority information is unclear
[ ] Incomplete delivery instructions

## After delivery

[ ] Delivery date recorded
[ ] Recipient confirmation received, if required
[ ] Follow-up or tracking item recorded
