The service

Individual Tax Preparation

Tax preparation begins with understanding the taxpayer, the year, and the records involved. Alchemize helps organize income documents, relevant deduction and credit records, prior-year information, and major changes before preparation and filing readiness.

Designed around the situation

Who this service is for.

A service conversation begins with what is happening—not with forcing every need into a template.

Often a fit for

  • Employees with one or multiple W-2s
  • Individuals with dependents, education costs, retirement income, investments, or homeownership records
  • People with self-employment or side-income records
  • Taxpayers who moved, married, divorced, or experienced another significant change

Common reasons to reach out

  • Documents have arrived from several employers or institutions
  • A prior-year return or tax notice needs to be organized
  • Self-employment income and business-related records need review
  • You want a clear missing-items list before preparation begins

Practical support

What Alchemize can help with.

The exact scope is confirmed before work begins.

  • Federal and applicable state return preparation
  • Income-document and prior-year record organization
  • Review of potentially relevant deductions and credits based on the information provided
  • Filing-readiness review and identification of missing information
  • Year-round document organization for the next filing season

What the process looks like

A clear path from question to next step.

  1. 01

    Gather

    Receive a tailored preparation list and collect records through the approved method.

  2. 02

    Review

    Organize documents, identify gaps, and discuss changes that may affect preparation.

  3. 03

    Prepare

    Prepare the return within the accepted engagement and clarify questions that arise.

  4. 04

    Finalize

    Review filing information, complete required authorizations, and confirm next steps.

Before we begin

What to prepare.

Bring context first. Sensitive records should be transmitted only through an approved method when Alchemize instructs you to do so.

  • Taxpayer, spouse, and dependent information
  • W-2, 1099, retirement, unemployment, Social Security, investment, and other income records
  • Education, childcare, charitable, property, and health-related documents where applicable
  • Self-employment income and applicable expense records
  • Prior-year return, estimated-payment records, and tax correspondence
