The service

Notary & Document Services

A notary verifies identity, willingness, and other requirements of an authorized notarial act. Alchemize helps clients prepare complete documents, required signers, acceptable identification, and requesting-party instructions before the appointment.

Designed around the situation

Who this service is for.

A service conversation begins with what is happening—not with forcing every need into a template.

Often a fit for

  • Individuals with documents requiring notarization
  • Signers coordinating witnesses or multiple participants
  • Clients who need help organizing personal documents
  • People responding to instructions from a requesting organization

Common reasons to reach out

  • A document must be signed in a notary's presence
  • Names or instructions need to be checked before the appointment
  • Witness or signer logistics need coordination
  • Completed documents need an organized return process

Practical support

What Alchemize can help with.

The exact scope is confirmed before work begins.

  • Perform authorized notarial acts where permitted
  • Review appointment-readiness requirements
  • Coordinate signer, witness, and identification reminders
  • Support general personal-document organization
  • Clarify delivery or return instructions supplied by the requesting party

What the process looks like

A clear path from question to next step.

  1. 01

    Prepare

    Confirm the document, act requested, signers, witnesses, and instructions.

  2. 02

    Verify

    Present acceptable identification and allow the notary to complete required verification.

  3. 03

    Execute

    Sign or acknowledge the document as required for the authorized act.

  4. 04

    Complete

    Finish the notarial certificate and follow the requesting organization's return instructions.

Before we begin

What to prepare.

Bring context first. Sensitive records should be transmitted only through an approved method when Alchemize instructs you to do so.

  • A complete document with blank spaces addressed
  • Acceptable, current identification
  • All required signers and witnesses
  • Instructions from the receiving or requesting organization
  • Payment and document return information where applicable

Important considerations

Clear professional boundaries.

A notary performs authorized notarial acts; a notary does not select legal forms, draft legal language, determine whether a document is appropriate, or provide legal advice unless separately authorized to do so.
