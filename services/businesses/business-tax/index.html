The service

Business Tax Services

Business tax preparation depends on the quality and completeness of the underlying records. Alchemize helps owners assemble relevant information, identify gaps, and prepare agreed business filings within the service scope.

Designed around the situation

Who this service is for.

A service conversation begins with what is happening—not with forcing every need into a template.

Often a fit for

  • Owners preparing annual business tax records
  • Businesses with contractor, payroll, asset, or mileage information
  • Entities organizing prior returns and formation records
  • Owners responding to tax correspondence

Common reasons to reach out

  • Income and expense records come from several systems
  • Bookkeeping reports need a readiness review
  • Contractor or payroll documents are incomplete
  • Prior returns, estimated payments, or notices need organization

Practical support

What Alchemize can help with.

The exact scope is confirmed before work begins.

  • Organize business income and expense documentation
  • Review bookkeeping-readiness and identify missing records
  • Assemble contractor, payroll, asset, vehicle, and entity information where applicable
  • Prepare agreed business tax filings within confirmed scope
  • Create a clear workflow for questions, authorizations, and final records

What the process looks like

A clear path from question to next step.

  1. 01

    Assemble

    Gather reports, records, prior filings, entity documents, and notices.

  2. 02

    Reconcile

    Identify gaps, inconsistencies, and questions requiring clarification.

  3. 03

    Prepare

    Complete the accepted business tax work using the records provided.

  4. 04

    Close

    Review required authorizations, filing steps, and records to retain.

Before we begin

What to prepare.

Bring context first. Sensitive records should be transmitted only through an approved method when Alchemize instructs you to do so.

  • Legal business and entity information
  • Income, expense, banking, and bookkeeping records
  • Payroll and contractor documentation where applicable
  • Asset, equipment, vehicle, and mileage records where applicable
  • Prior returns, estimated payments, and tax correspondence
