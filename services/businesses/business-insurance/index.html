The service

Business Insurance Solutions

Coverage discussions begin with how the business actually operates. Alchemize helps organize the business profile, existing policies, contractual requirements, and practical exposures that shape available insurance options.

Designed around the situation

Who this service is for.

A service conversation begins with what is happening—not with forcing every need into a template.

Often a fit for

  • New businesses preparing for an insurance discussion
  • Owners approaching renewal
  • Businesses whose operations, staff, locations, or equipment have changed
  • Companies responding to contractual insurance requirements

Common reasons to reach out

  • Current limits or deductibles are unclear
  • New employees, contractors, vehicles, property, or services change the exposure
  • A customer or contract requests proof of coverage
  • Existing coverage needs an organized review

Practical support

What Alchemize can help with.

The exact scope is confirmed before work begins.

  • Document business activities, locations, people, equipment, vehicles, and customer interaction
  • Organize current policies, limits, deductibles, and renewal dates
  • Identify contractual requirements and priority questions
  • Explain common terminology and available categories within licensed scope
  • Prepare information for application and underwriting discussions

What the process looks like

A clear path from question to next step.

  1. 01

    Profile

  4. 04

Before we begin

What to prepare.

Bring context first. Sensitive records should be transmitted only through an approved method when Alchemize instructs you to do so.

  • Legal name, entity type, industry, products/services, locations, and years operating
  • Employees, contractors, equipment, vehicles, and customer locations
  • Current policy types, carrier, limits, deductibles, and renewal dates
  • Property, liability, professional, employee, vehicle, and contractual exposures
  • Coverage goals and questions
