The service

Notary & Administrative Services

Business documents may require both a valid notarial act and a clear administrative workflow. Alchemize separates those functions: the notary performs authorized acts, while administrative support can organize instructions, signers, copies, delivery, and recordkeeping.

Designed around the situation

Who this service is for.

A service conversation begins with what is happening—not with forcing every need into a template.

Often a fit for

  • Businesses coordinating document execution
  • Owners managing recurring forms and records
  • Teams with multiple signers or witnesses
  • Companies responding to requesting-organization instructions

Common reasons to reach out

  • A business document requires notarization
  • Signer authority or capacity information needs organization
  • Copies, return instructions, and follow-up records are inconsistent
  • Recurring document work needs a clearer process

Practical support

What Alchemize can help with.

The exact scope is confirmed before work begins.

  • Perform authorized notarial acts where permitted
  • Coordinate document, signer, witness, identification, and appointment readiness
  • Organize business forms, copies, delivery instructions, and follow-up records
  • Create a practical recurring-document workflow
  • Support general business record organization

What the process looks like

A clear path from question to next step.

  1. 01

    Prepare

    Confirm the document, requested act, authority, signers, witnesses, and instructions.

  2. 02

    Verify

    Review required identification and complete the notary's verification steps.

  3. 03

    Execute

    Complete signatures or acknowledgments required for the authorized act.

  4. 04

    Record

    Follow delivery instructions and file copies or related records appropriately.

Before we begin

What to prepare.

Bring context first. Sensitive records should be transmitted only through an approved method when Alchemize instructs you to do so.

  • Complete document and requesting-party instructions
  • All signers, titles, capacities, and authority information
  • Acceptable identification and required witnesses
  • Supporting business information and copies
  • Delivery, return, and follow-up recordkeeping instructions

Important considerations

Clear professional boundaries.

Notarial acts, administrative support, and legal services are separate. Alchemize is not a law firm and does not select legal documents, draft legal language, determine signer authority as a legal matter, or provide legal advice.
