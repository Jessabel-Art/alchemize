01

Introduction and scope

Alchemize Business Services LLC (“Alchemize,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects the privacy of visitors to getalchemize.com. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, disclosed, and protected when visitors use this public website or communicate with Alchemize through it.

This Policy concerns the current public website. It does not describe a client portal, payment system, secure document platform, or other system that is not presently offered. If a professional-service engagement involves separate systems, forms, disclosures, or agreements, additional privacy terms may apply.

02

Information we collect

Information you provide directly

The website provides a consultation-inquiry form. Information submitted through that form may be collected and stored so Alchemize can respond to inquiries, evaluate requested services, communicate with prospective clients, and maintain appropriate business records. The form requests:

  • first and last name;
  • email address;
  • an optional telephone number;
  • the general service area related to the inquiry;
  • a general message; and
  • an optional preferred contact method.

You may also voluntarily provide information when communicating with Alchemize by email or another method made available to you. Please provide only information reasonably necessary for the inquiry.

Do not send sensitive records through ordinary channels

Do not submit Social Security numbers, tax returns, banking credentials, payment-card information, insurance identification numbers, government identification documents, medical information, passwords, or confidential business records through a general website form or unsecured email unless Alchemize specifically instructs you to use an approved secure method. The website does not currently offer a secure upload portal.

Technical information

Like most public websites, the hosting and infrastructure used to deliver the site may automatically generate ordinary technical logs. Depending on the provider and configuration, those logs may include an IP address, browser or device information, referring page, requested pages, and the date and time of a request. Alchemize does not currently use this website to build advertising profiles about visitors.

03

How information may be used

Alchemize may use information, as appropriate, to:

  • respond to an inquiry or requested communication;
  • understand which service area may fit the stated need;
  • communicate about a consultation or next step;
  • provide requested information and maintain related business records;
  • operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the website;
  • prevent misuse and protect legal rights; and
  • comply with applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

Communications

Responses to inquiries and service-related messages are different from promotional marketing. The website does not currently provide a newsletter or marketing-email signup. If Alchemize later introduces promotional email, the applicable notice and opt-out process will be addressed when that functionality is implemented.

04

Sharing and disclosure

Alchemize may disclose information only where reasonably appropriate for the purpose involved, including to website hosting or infrastructure providers; technology or operational vendors acting on Alchemize’s behalf; professional advisers where appropriate; authorities or other parties when required by law; or parties involved in protecting rights, safety, and site integrity.

Information may also be addressed as part of a merger, reorganization, financing, sale, or transfer of some or all business assets, subject to applicable law and appropriate handling of the information.

No sale for monetary consideration

As part of the website’s current operations, Alchemize does not sell personal information for monetary consideration. This statement describes current business practice and is not intended as a conclusion about every statutory definition of “sale” or “sharing.”

05

Cookies, analytics, and tracking

Current website use

Alchemize has not implemented advertising pixels, behavioral advertising, audience profiling, or a dedicated analytics platform on the current website. The site does not use browser local storage or session storage. It requests font files from Google Fonts, which may receive ordinary technical request information when those files are loaded.

The hosting provider may use technically necessary mechanisms or server logs to deliver and protect the website. Because no current site feature requires an Alchemize-managed consent banner, one has not been added.

Future technologies

If analytics, functionality cookies, scheduling, payments, or other technologies are introduced later, Alchemize will evaluate the related notice, consent, and policy requirements and update this Policy where appropriate before or when those features are enabled.

06

Retention and security

Retention

Alchemize retains information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, legitimate business and recordkeeping needs, dispute resolution, security, and applicable legal requirements. Practices may vary according to the type of information, the communication, and any later professional-service engagement. No universal deletion period is promised by this Policy.

Security

Alchemize seeks to use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the information and the systems in use. No method of Internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed absolutely secure. Visitors should not use ordinary public forms or unsecured email to transmit sensitive records.

Security incidents

If a security incident occurs, Alchemize will assess and address it and provide notices where required by applicable law. This Policy does not promise a notification schedule beyond applicable requirements.

07

Your choices and privacy rights

You may choose not to provide information through the website, although that may limit Alchemize’s ability to respond. You may also request that Alchemize correct contact information or stop a communication that is not required for an active inquiry, service, recordkeeping need, or legal obligation.

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have rights concerning personal information, potentially including access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability. Those rights are not identical in every jurisdiction and may be subject to verification, exceptions, or legal limits. Requests may be sent to founder@getalchemize.com. Alchemize will evaluate a request under the law that applies to the circumstances.

08

Children’s privacy

The website concerns professional services and is not directed to children under 13. Alchemize does not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13 through the website. If Alchemize learns that such information was submitted in circumstances requiring action, it may take appropriate steps to remove it.

09

Third-party services and links

The current site uses third-party font delivery and will rely on website hosting and infrastructure. Other vendors may be introduced only as business functionality is implemented. Possible future categories include scheduling, analytics, email delivery, payment processing, and secure document systems; this list does not mean those services are currently connected.

The website may link to external sites for convenience or information. Those sites control their own content and privacy practices. Alchemize is not responsible for a third party’s privacy practices, security, or content, and visitors should review the third party’s policies.

10

International visitors

The website is operated for a United States business and may be accessible elsewhere. Visitors outside the United States understand that information they choose to provide may be processed in the United States, subject to applicable law. This statement does not create a specific international-transfer guarantee.

11

Changes to this Policy

Alchemize may update this Policy as website functionality, service providers, legal requirements, or business operations evolve. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective or last-updated date. Individual email notice will not necessarily be provided for every change.
