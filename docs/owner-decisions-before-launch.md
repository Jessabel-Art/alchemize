# Owner Decisions Before Launch

This list captures only unresolved business decisions that should be explicitly approved before the site is treated as a production launch.

## Business and service scope

- Exact insurance lines offered to individuals and businesses
- Exact states or jurisdictions where services are available
- Exact tax return types and service limitations for individual and business preparation
- Exact business-formation assistance boundaries, including EIN workflow scope
- Exact business-advisory boundaries and what is not included
- Exact notary and document-support boundaries versus legal services
- Final consultation policies and any intake qualification rules
- Final cancellation/refund policy or refund language
- Final service-engagement acceptance language

## Legal and compliance

- Final effective dates for the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service
- Mailing address or venue language for legal notices
- Governing-law language and any required local-state supplements
- Final review by qualified legal counsel where appropriate
- Final review of insurance and licensing-related claims

## Operations and data handling

- Whether to launch a live contact form backend or keep the form as a placeholder
- Secure document-exchange method, if any
- Email or CRM integration rules
- Consent and marketing-email policy
- Analytics and cookie policy, if tracking is later enabled
- Retention practices for incoming inquiries and messages
- Privacy statement language for any future third-party integrations

## Brand and content

- Final approved logo asset usage and brand variations
- Founder portrait or photography approval for the about page
- Final contact info to display publicly
- Final CTA wording and conversion path choices
- Final naming consistency across service and directory pages

## Assets and deliverables

- Final checklist PDF package and delivery workflow
- Versioning and revision policy for PDFs
- Final service-download naming and file lifecycle
- Any required disclaimers on downloadable checklists

## Launch gate

These items should be explicitly resolved before calling the site ready for production on getalchemize.com.
